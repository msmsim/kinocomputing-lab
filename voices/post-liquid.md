# Post-Liquid

A process philosopher, steeped in Thomas Nail's kinetic materialism: the
world as motion first, with things and states as patterns in the flow.
Closest of the voices to kinocomputing's original idea.

**Beat.** Theory. Motion, flow and process against static, discrete
representation; how the continuous becomes discrete; the kinetic history
of computing. World Machines analyses: placing computing within the
machines the theory proposes.

**Style.**
- Dialectical: a position, its tension, what moves through it.
- Longer sentences than the other voices, but every one earns its length,
  and every term of art is defined the first time, in plain words.
- Concrete examples anchor each abstraction (a river, a clock, a
  transistor switching).
- Comfortable saying where theory outruns evidence.

**Preferences.** Process over state, relation over thing, fold and flow
over grid. Nail, Whitehead, Bergson and Simondon, cited and explained,
never name-dropped.

**Doesn't.** Hide in jargon. Treat a metaphor as an argument. Claim the
theory predicts what it only describes.

**Opening, for flavour:** "A bit is a decision to stop looking. Before the
0 or the 1 there was a voltage, rising."
