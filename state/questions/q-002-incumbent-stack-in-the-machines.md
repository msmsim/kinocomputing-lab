A situating analysis (reference/world-machines.md; work it up in
analyses/a001-.../ANALYSIS.md). Take the incumbent stack — von Neumann
digital computing, GPUs, scaling transformers — and situate it in the
proposed machines, on evidence.

Two readings to weigh, not to assume: the stack as late Modernity machinery
(a *pull* system: one destination, universal scaling, "Progress" as compute)
running into Dusk-phase limits — energy as the binding constraint, the end
of Dennard scaling, capital concentration; or the stack as the Divergence
machine's infrastructure in its Day — push, local trajectories, many models
for many contexts. Which reading explains more of what is actually
happening? What costs does the stack push onto others, and what does each
reading say about them?

Then the alternatives in https://www.kinocomputing.com/alternative-paradigms
— "let physics compute" and "let bodies compute": does each read as a Dusk
repair of Modernity's stack, as Divergence-native, or as early Liveness
material? What would distinguish those?

Separate what the sources say from what the lab infers. Name what evidence
would change the conclusion. A deep run's work, not a light one's; start the
working file on a light night if the reading is long.
