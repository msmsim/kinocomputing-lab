https://www.kinocomputing.com/alternative-paradigms gives each of ~25 paradigms
a "Latest" line. When a light night has nothing better to do, check one
paradigm for a significant development since that note, and publish a short,
sourced notebook entry on it. One paradigm per night at most; skip anything
that isn't a real development (a funding round is not one; a measured result
is).
