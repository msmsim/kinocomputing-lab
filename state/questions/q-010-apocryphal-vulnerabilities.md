From the PI (2026-11-23), after the PI's note on apocryphal vulnerabilities
(https://www.msweet.net/notes/164-crimson-kites): a field's authority often
rests partly on a founding anecdote, quotation, statistic or single study —
"the brain runs on 20 watts", "a slime mould redesigned the Tokyo rail
network", Landauer's limit as the floor that matters — and when such a story
proves false or overstretched, what was built on it is exposed. Correction
usually takes decades, because everyone cites and nobody re-checks.

The lab treats hunting these as **standing debt**, like technical debt: a
backlog it pays down steadily, and a service to anyone working in these
fields. The ledger is `audits/foundations.md` (AGENT.md, "The foundations
audit").
