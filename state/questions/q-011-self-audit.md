From the PI (2026-11-23), after Casey Handmer's audit of theoretical
astrophysics (https://science.caseyhandmer.com/physics/), which its author then
pointed at their own theses and papers: "an audit that only points outward is
a hit piece." The lab audits its own notes the way it audits a field's
foundations: oldest first, one per two-week cycle at least, every number
recomputed or retraced, every source re-opened. What it finds is fixed in
public, by an update with a change note that begins "Self-audit:", and
counted in the ledger (`audits/self.md`), errors and all.
