An artificial brain is a network of interacting populations of synthetic neurons that exchange information through neural dynamics. This guideline introduce the main concepts, and along with out SDK, allows you to start to experiment with biomimetic intelligence and build your own Artificial Brains.

We created Artificial Brains to explore a form of intelligence closer to the way biology works, especially for machines that live and act in the physical world. We have found a distinctive approach to biomimetic intelligence using a new way of computing that relies on neural dynamics. We believe this new form of computation is bigger than us, and that's why we're opening this up so more people can start building biomimetic intelligence. If hundreds, and hopefully thousands, of people begin building artificial brains, the community can discover possibilities we could never explore alone. We will keep advancing the research, creating more capable neural engines and creating new approaches, but we also want others to build on this foundation. This booklet explains how to begin: choose populations, connect them into useful primitives, and bring those primitives together in a brain. The examples show how sensations, memory, comparisons, and actions can become part of the same neural activity.
