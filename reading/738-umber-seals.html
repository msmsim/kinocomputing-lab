I built Evidence Guide because audits of research papers are scattered across websites and individual projects. It’s hard to find out what has already been checked, compare the methods, or know whether you’re repeating someone else’s work.

Look up a DOI or PMID, or search by title or author. Title and author search currently covers six starter papers and other listed papers with verified metadata. Public records are free to browse without an account. The collection is small and growing. A missing result doesn’t mean a paper has passed checks.
