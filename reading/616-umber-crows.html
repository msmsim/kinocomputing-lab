Hot-state dependency: A condition in which a system's ability to function depends on organizational relationships, accumulated state, or operational knowledge that exists only in its current running configuration and cannot be reliably reconstructed following interruption. Yes, I'm thinking about the Microsoft cloud outage that's going on right now. It could be recoverable, still most likely is, but it could also be the harbinger of a new category of systemic disaster. Heat death: What happens when a system with hot-state dependency crashes and can't be recovered.
