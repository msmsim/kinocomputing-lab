You can’t “1000x” complex systems. You can only do that to singular legibilized variables.

So the hard problem in AI can be defined as follows: How do you get larger workgroups and orgs with a rich plurality of perspectives working on technological stacks with high fabricatory depth and strong shearing across pace layers working together effectively in an AI-native way?

It’s fascinating that this is a group of a dozen-something people coordinating far more tightly than most corporate teams of similar size 10 years ago. I used to run a team of about a dozen people at Xerox circa 2010-11. The blygger contributor group has collectively done 10x the work that team did over 2 years. This isn’t a solipsistic reality tunnel or an auteur project. It started diverging (in a good way) from my initial vision of it within a week of announcement, as other people began contributing from diverse perspectives. Fortunately for me I don’t know enough to even try to exercise auteur control over what’s going on, which might be a good starting condition.
