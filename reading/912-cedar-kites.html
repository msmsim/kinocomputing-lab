TL;DR We built InsiderBench, 500 questions and answer rubrics from long podcast interviews with 100 practitioners in 20 industries. Six frontier models cover fewer than half of the rubric points, and 43% of the points are missed by all six. Web search barely helps. What the models miss is what practitioners learn by doing the work and talking to people. LLMs are blind outsiders. To close the gap, models need to be exposed to more insider information, in pretraining or in environments at test time. Welcome to the Era of Insider Information.

We introduce InsiderBench, a dataset of 500 questions from long podcast interviews with 100 practitioners in 20 industries, from bankers and chip designers to chefs and physicists. Each question comes with three or four rubric points taken from the practitioner’s own answer.

From each interview we picked five moments where the guest explains how something works, and wrote each as a plain question without naming the guest. We selected questions that lean toward how the world works, to reduce opinionated prescriptions.
