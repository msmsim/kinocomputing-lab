Situate our different paradigms in the regimes mentioned here and also eval their general playability.

In the top left quadrant, hard and analytic, are things like formal proofs and verified computations. Once the formal setting is fixed, the relation is unforgiving. In the top right, hard and constitutive, are things like cryptographic hardness as civilization actually depends on it. SHA-256 is completely specified, but the claim we practically care about—that adversaries cannot feasibly invert it—is something we continue to inhabit through adversarial history. We do not possess a little certificate from God saying that no sufficiently ingenious mathematician will ever surprise us. Oozy and analytic regimes include many fluid systems. Weather is gloriously oozy, but also astonishingly modellable. Huge continuous fields can be represented well enough for predictions that would have looked supernatural a century ago. Oozy and constitutive regimes include friction in robotics, social situations, ecological responses, and many varieties of practical skill where the detailed structure matters but often reveals its relevance only as things unfold.

There is always a playability frontier beyond which lies a constitutive realm of chopping wood and carrying water, regardless of the nature and power of the intelligence and the transcendent chapters of its life.

If your whole personality actually is being a racecar driver (the theme of a strange HP Formula 1 AI ad incidentally), there isn’t much hope for you. If you identify with a domain of playable prowess, you can only ever self-actualize as an It that will forever fall short of the self-driving race car. But if being a race car driver merely supplies an intersubjective relational ground for a richer mode of being, there is hope for you out there, in the vast ocean of constitutive realities, past the shores of the island of race-car driving role-playable personalities. The Kegan fans out there can restate this idea in Kegan-level terms.

Inside highly playable regimes, intelligence can move very fast, and prowess-based identities can develop and harden very fast. Once a feedback signal is cheap and reliable, learning can proceed at the speed of compute, for both human brains and computers. Millions of attempts can be generated, checked, discarded, and recombined.
