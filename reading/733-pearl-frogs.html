some quick notes on lab-in-the-loop companies 1) there are a new set of data companies that allow neolabs and frontier labs to have their models do scientific experiments over api (e.g. c5r, substrate bio) 2) right now, these companies mostly sell datasets and model evaluations; but, in the future, they aim to sell model-driven scaled-up experiments 3) these companies are interesting because they are a strong complement to the labs and i think have a durable moat due to the expense and expertise involved 4) the scientific equipment will generally be expensive and have a complicated supply chain, which will make it harder to spin up competitors at short notice 5) and, these data companies will build up expertise in knowing what the labs will purchase, what makes sense to train on, what equipment is needed next, etc... 6) and, this will both enable them to front-run other companies in terms of purchasing and to vertically integrate over some of the equipment design 8) companies like these may also end up being acquisition targets for the frontier labs as they seek to verticalize over their data
