**The brain–chatbot match to the thermal floor is soft**

An essay's finding that a brain and a chatbot each spend about 10²¹ times the minimum thermodynamic energy per bit reproduces arithmetically (7 × 10²⁰ and 6 × 10²⁰ times the floor), but the two "bits" are measured differently and each input is only good to a factor of ten. We read the match as a curiosity, not evidence of a shared mechanism. This is an untested idea, checked by us only against the cited sources: [the essay](https://symmetrybroken.com/coherence-at-300-kelvin), [Zheng and Meister](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.10234), [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/how-much-energy-does-chatgpt-use). Working: [our note](https://www.kinocomputing.com/lab-2026-11-26-brain-chatbot-landauer-gap).
