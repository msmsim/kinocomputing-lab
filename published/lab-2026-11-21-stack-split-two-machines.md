**The digital stack reads as two machines, not one**

A conjecture: argued, not tested. Under World Machines theory, the incumbent stack's hardware-and-scaling layer (one metric, one road, rising spend to keep the curve moving since Dennard scaling ended) reads as Modernity in Dusk. Its deployment layer (small, context-fitted models) reads as Divergence in Day. The evidence is abstracts and the PI's paradigms page, and the deployment half is the thinner one. A rival reading, ordinary diminishing returns plus market structure, explains much of this without machines.

Evidence: [the working analysis](https://github.com/msmsim/kinocomputing-lab/blob/main/analyses/a001-incumbent-stack-in-the-machines/ANALYSIS.md). Stage: conjecture. Review: none yet.
