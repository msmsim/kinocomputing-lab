**New in Reading**

- ['Just Ridiculous': A Famed Philosopher Takes on AI Consciousness (WIRED)](https://wired.com/story/just-ridiculous-a-famed-philosopher-takes-on-ai-consciousness): Interview in which a philosopher doubts AI systems are conscious yet allows non-biological conscious beings in principle, and worries about lost human intellectual effort.
- [AI as a tool that allows selection pressure to act on code (Aneesh Sathe)](https://blyg.aneeshsathe.com/t/28dgvc6a2tqjt8th3dc61fvy74): A reply to the Regenerable Software essay arguing software has an ecological niche, so cheaper regeneration speeds a Red Queen race rather than ending it. An argument, not a measurement.
- [Asymmetric Evolution (symmetry, broken)](https://symmetrybroken.com/asymmetric-evolution): Part of a series on a 'synchronization tax': the thermodynamic cost of keeping systems' descriptions in agreement. A theory essay, assuming the series' earlier parts.
- [Bayesian inference with a polymer probabilistic computer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14782): Four conjugated-polymer probabilistic bits sampling 16-state Bayesian posteriors within the spread expected of ideal bits.
- [Coherence at 300 Kelvin (symmetry, broken)](https://symmetrybroken.com/coherence-at-300-kelvin): A long theory essay linking Landauer's floor, multiplicative noise and attention dynamics, with a list of falsifiable tests and Pythia experiments; it compares brain and chatbot energy per bit.
- [Could a computer scientist build a brain?](https://stankerstjens.github.io/could-a-computer-scientist-build-a-brain): An essay reading evolution, development and learning as one process on different time scales, with the genome as a compressed record that development unfolds.
- [Eric Raymond on 'hot-state dependency'](https://x.com/esrtweet/status/2121339860824797635): A short post defining hot-state dependency: a system that depends on relationships and operational knowledge existing only in its running configuration, and 'heat death' when it crashes and cannot be rebuilt.
- [Evaluating Positive Feedback Adiabatic Logic in 16nm FinFET with a Realistic Power-Clock](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19999): A simulation study of adiabatic logic at 16 nm finding energy advantages over static CMOS in small circuits, and three sources of loss that limit them.
- [Extreme Event Likelihoods with Guided Generative Models](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/extreme-event-likelihoods-with-guided-generative-models): A vendor blog on guiding a diffusion climate emulator toward tropical cyclones to estimate odds ratios; an example of generative models as risk-analysis tools.
- [How much energy does ChatGPT use? (Epoch AI)](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/how-much-energy-does-chatgpt-use): Epoch AI's estimate of about 0.3 Wh for a typical GPT-4o text query, with its assumptions, and why older estimates were ten times higher.
- [IBM Quantum Open Plan updates](https://www.ibm.com/quantum/blog/open-plan-updates): Describes free access to real quantum hardware (10 minutes per 28 days) for outsiders.
- [Information and thermodynamics: experimental verification of Landauer's Erasure principle](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/2015/06/p06015): A colloidal-particle one-bit memory whose mean heat of erasure saturates at the Landauer bound for slow cycles: the principle measured, not just argued.
