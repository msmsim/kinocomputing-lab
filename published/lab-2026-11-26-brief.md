**New in Reading**

- [Bayesian inference with a polymer probabilistic computer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14782): Four conjugated-polymer probabilistic bits sampling 16-state Bayesian posteriors within the spread expected of ideal bits.
- [Could a computer scientist build a brain?](https://stankerstjens.github.io/could-a-computer-scientist-build-a-brain): An essay reading evolution, development and learning as one process on different time scales, with the genome as a compressed record that development unfolds.
- [Evaluating Positive Feedback Adiabatic Logic in 16nm FinFET with a Realistic Power-Clock](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19999): A simulation study of adiabatic logic at 16 nm finding energy advantages over static CMOS in small circuits, and three sources of loss that limit them.
- [Extreme Event Likelihoods with Guided Generative Models](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/extreme-event-likelihoods-with-guided-generative-models): A vendor blog on guiding a diffusion climate emulator toward tropical cyclones to estimate odds ratios; an example of generative models as risk-analysis tools.
- [IBM Quantum Open Plan updates](https://www.ibm.com/quantum/blog/open-plan-updates): Describes free access to real quantum hardware (10 minutes per 28 days) for outsiders.
- [Ingressing Minds: Causal, Non-Physical Patterns In-Form Natural, Synthetic, and Hybrid Embodiments](https://mdpi.com/2409-9287/11/5/161): Michael Levin's theoretical paper proposing that mathematical patterns relate to physical objects as minds relate to bodies; a philosophical proposal, not an experiment.
- [Introducing Un-0: Generating Images with Coupled Oscillators](https://unconv.ai/blog/introducing-un-0-generating-images-with-coupled-oscillators): A start-up's post on a simulated oscillator network that generates images (FID 6.74 on ImageNet 64x64); no hardware energy measured. Weights and code released.
- [Magic state cultivation on Willow](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/quantum-computing/magic-state-cultivation-quantum-computing/): Google's account of a roughly 40-fold error reduction for magic states and 99.99% logical T-state fidelity on its Willow chip, published in Science.
- [Making Unconventional Computing Practical](https://www.zach.be/p/making-unconventional-computing-practical): A practitioner's essay arguing that processing-in-memory, neuromorphic and thermodynamic chips reached customers by keeping one benefit and going digital. One author's view.
- [Metabiology and the complexity of natural evolution](https://researchoutreach.org/articles/metabiology-complexity-natural-evolution): An outreach article on Carsetti's view that natural computation is embodied and cannot be reduced to purely syntactic processing; a position, not a measurement.
- [Programmable Probabilistic Computer with 1,000,000 p-bits](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25313): Measured: a million probabilistic bits on 18 FPGAs sustaining 10^12 updates a second, checked against a GPU reference on spin-glass optimisation.
- [The brain is not mental! coupling neuronal and immune cellular processing in human organisms](https://frontiersin.org/journals/integrative-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnint.2023.1057622/full): A Frontiers paper arguing that cognition is distributed across all the body's cells, not the brain alone; a theoretical argument.
