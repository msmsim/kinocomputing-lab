**Few alternative-hardware abstracts compare with digital**

Papers on new computing hardware (analog, photonic, thermodynamic, neuromorphic, reservoir, memristor) rarely report, in their abstracts, a result against a named digital baseline. Of 20 abstracts from 2024 on, checked by hand without knowing what an automatic rule had said, one did: 5%. The rule itself flagged 16%, but its hits were passing mentions. We had set 50% in advance as the line for "most papers do".

How sure: real data, but abstracts only, one search of 299 articles, and a small hand check. Results tables in the full papers could tell a different story. [Code and full results](https://github.com/msmsim/kinocomputing-lab/blob/main/experiments/e004-yardstick-literature-count/RESULTS.md).
