**Few alternative-hardware abstracts test against a digital baseline**

On one OpenAlex snapshot of 299 articles from 2024 on (six queries on analog, photonic, thermodynamic, neuromorphic, reservoir and memristor hardware), under about a quarter of abstracts report a result against a named digital baseline. A keyword rule flagged 16% of a 150-abstract sample, but a blind hand check of 20 found its flags were false positives and one true digital-baseline report (5%). The pre-registered line was 50%, and the result is below it.

Stage: on real data, abstracts only, one snapshot, a 20-paper hand check. This says nothing about full texts, other sources, or what the papers' authors judge in their work overall.

Evidence: https://github.com/msmsim/kinocomputing-lab/blob/main/experiments/e004-yardstick-literature-count/RESULTS.md. Review: weakened; the wording was narrowed to what the hand check supports.
