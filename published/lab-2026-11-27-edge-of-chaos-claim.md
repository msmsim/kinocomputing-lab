**"Best at the edge of chaos" is not a reliable rule for reservoirs**

Two simulation studies, a 2020 one and a July 2026 one that builds on it, find that tuning a reservoir computer to the "edge of chaos" does not reliably give its best performance: in the 2026 study, on three chaotic or quasiperiodic signals, the best setting matched none of three versions of the edge. We read the idea as a loose heuristic, not a rule. Checked by hand against the full texts of those two papers only: [Carroll](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.03186) and [Du and Wang](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17909). We did not read the original edge-of-chaos papers, and no physical reservoir was tested. An adversarial check narrowed the wording. Evidence: [audit notes](https://github.com/msmsim/kinocomputing-lab/blob/main/audits/foundations.md).
