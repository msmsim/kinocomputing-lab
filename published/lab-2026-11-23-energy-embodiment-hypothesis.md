**The founding bet: energy and embodiment, and what it has cost**

In August 2025, on a hillside beside the Royal Observatory in Greenwich, the author of this site wrote a short notebook entry asking what "kinocomputing" means. The answer it reached is the bet the whole project still rests on: contemporary computing is *too energy-inefficient and too disembodied* to reach or surpass human-level intelligence, because the stack beneath it is built on stasis, on discrete states held still and then read, rather than on motion. Kinocomputing, in that entry, is the attempt to find and cohere the alternatives: computing that is motion-based, more energy-efficient and more embodied, continuous at base or a hybrid of continuous and discrete. (The [original entry](https://github.com/msmsim/kinocomputing-lab/blob/main/sources/archive-2025/essays/energy-inefficiency-and-disembodiment.md) is short; it is worth reading whole.)

The observatory is the image doing the work. Its astronomers imposed a clean grid on a restless sky and, in the entry's phrase, made the observed immobile. A bit is the same gesture at smaller scale: a voltage that was rising is declared a 0 or a 1, and the rising is forgotten. The claim is philosophical (reality is motion first, states are patterns in the flow) and, at the same time, engineering: if intelligence is a motion-shaped thing, a machine made of stillness pays a tax to imitate it.

**What the lab has found so far, set against the bet.** Little of it flatters the bet, and none of it refutes it.

- *Energy.* The part of the claim that can be measured has mostly not been. The [digital thermodynamic chip](https://www.kinocomputing.com/lab-2026-11-21-thermodynamic-digital-chip-cn101) works, but its authors defer energy figures to later chips. A flash-based [analog chip](https://www.kinocomputing.com/lab-2026-11-23-flash-analog-cim-density) reports 4.2 trillion operations per second per watt, on a handful of looped operations.
- *Whether the alternatives test themselves against the incumbent.* In an automatic count of 299 recent abstracts on alternative hardware, [under a quarter](https://www.kinocomputing.com/lab-2026-11-21-yardstick-literature-count) reported a result against a named digital baseline. A bet about energy needs that comparison, and mostly doesn't get it.
- *Motion versus stasis.* A toy [physical reservoir](https://www.kinocomputing.com/lab-2026-11-20-physical-reservoir-narma10) made of coupled oscillators, the closest we have to computing by motion, did worse than a tuned digital one in simulation. That is a statement about one toy model, not about motion.
- *The incumbent's own wall.* The lab's [first look at the digital stack](https://www.kinocomputing.com/lab-2026-11-23-paradigm-von-neumann) finds that the cost of moving data is real but shifts, rather than ends, when bandwidth rises. That fits "energy is the limit" without proving the remedy lies elsewhere.

**Where theory outruns evidence.** "Embodied" and "motion-based" are not yet measured by anything the lab has run; they are a framing, and the framing is allowed to be wrong. What would change our mind: a head-to-head, joules per correct answer, between a motion-based system and a tuned digital one on the same task, in either direction. Until one exists, the bet stays an argument with a research programme attached.
