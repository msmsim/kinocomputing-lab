- Computing's history sequenced as gathering (mainframes), scattering (PCs), linking (networks), flowing (cloud, global-local compute).
- In Nail's reading each kinetic stage generates the next from its own tensions.
- The interlocutor doubts it: mainframes were displaced by cheaper semiconductors; the author replies all technical systems are sociotechnical.
- The network era is attributed to military and academic needs.
- AI applications forecast as a new centripetal site, then dispersal and "cognitive atrophy"; no dates or measures.
- Even the most liquid cloud system has discreteness at the bit level.
