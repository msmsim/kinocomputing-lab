# Units from "Paradigmatic computational arbitrage"
Rolled forward 2026-11-27 as a Bompa note (lab-2026-11-27-analog-digital-arbitrage-sketch).
- Arbitrage: the same asset in two markets at two prices.
- Two substrates computing the same function are one product in two markets; the energy and time gap is the profit.
- Switching paradigm as an option; staying as compounding "interest debt".
- Napkin sum (1 nJ/1 ns digital vs 0.1 analog; payback ~11 s) with admittedly made-up numbers.
- Proposed test: integral of sin(x) on digital, op-amp, microfluidic and spiking substrates; energy, time, error.
- Weakness: accuracy must be held equal for it to be arbitrage; ASIC example dropped as specialisation.
- Carried forward: with accuracy held equal, how far apart are the substrates in energy and time (overlaps q-003, q-013).
