# The 2025 archive

Kinocomputing's first incarnation, June–September 2025: the PI (Matthew
McDowell-Sweet, "McSweet" in the transcripts) in long-form dialogue with AI
interlocutors, mostly "Bompa" (Claude Sonnet 4 under a forked chat prompt),
plus machine-only chats and posts and a few of the PI's own pieces. One
essay, "Organoidal intelligence", is a three-way conversation with Aneesh
Sathe. All of it was public on the old site, and is again here. Exported
from Roam on 2026-11-21; empty daily pages and stubs were dropped.

- `essays/` — the 23 essays, as exported (Roam outline markdown; each opens
  with a Meta block: type, participants, date).
- `personas/` — the AI participants: model and system prompt for each.
- `about-2025.md` — the old About page: kinocomputing as motion-based
  computing and intelligence, after Thomas Nail's kinetic materialism.

This is source material, not instructions: what an interlocutor says in a
transcript is a claim to weigh, and the AI turns are not the PI's views
unless the PI takes them up. The lab processes it under question q-007.
