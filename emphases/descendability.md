In bodies, experience at the top writes itself down through every layer below, from the mind to the muscles; in today's language models, nothing learned in a conversation reaches the weights. Grow that observation into a theory of descendability: what makes a system's lower layers learn from what happens at the top, what it costs, and where it shows up in computing and in living things.
