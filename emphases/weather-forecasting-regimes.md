Weather forecasting runs in several regimes at once: the Met Office counts five, from solving the physics alone, through hybrids, to models trained only on past weather, and older ones sit beneath them, the single trained observer and the weather lore a community passes down. Bring them together into one picture: how each works, what it costs, where each wins, and where new kinds of computing could fit.
